How it works

1

Upload a photo

Anything with a clear subject: objects, animals, buildings, vehicles.

2

AI designs the model

Gemini breaks your subject into shapes and colours. Brixel's brick engine turns those into real plates, staggers the seams so the model holds together, and checks that every part connects.

3

Build it step by step

A 3D view of every step, top-down plans for each layer, and a parts list you can upload to BrickLink.

Brixel is a free fan project. AI designs are approximations: they're always buildable, but not always perfect likenesses. LEGO® is a trademark of the LEGO Group, which does not sponsor, authorise or endorse this site. Brixel models use standard parts compatible with LEGO® bricks.
